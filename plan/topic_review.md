# Topic review — all 90 ideas

Rule 1 of the channel: only verified content. I checked every idea against what the research says. **Verdicts:** *Keep* = accurate as phrased; *Reframe* = the effect is real but the phrasing overclaimed or the evidence is contested, so the angle changes; *Replace* = could not be verified or duplicated another day, so a verified topic replaces it.

Items marked **(verified in this review)** were confirmed against sources on the web today; everything else is re-verified sentence by sentence when its script is written (see each day's `sources.md`).

### Biggest accuracy fixes in your ideas

- **'First impressions form in 7 seconds'** (Day 43): no source; the research (Willis & Todorov 2006) says 100 milliseconds.
- **'Playing hard to get'** (Day 39): five experiments found it does NOT make you more attractive; being *selective* does.
- **'Sunday scaries'** (Day 61): Gallup data show Sunday mood equals Saturday; there is minimal Blue-Monday effect.
- **'5-second rule'** (Day 65), **'habit stacking'** (Day 67), **'2-minute rule'** (Day 62): productivity-book ideas without lab tests; reframed or replaced.
- **'Baader-Meinhof'** (Day 11): an internet folk name; scientists/linguists say 'frequency illusion'.
- **'66 days to form a habit'** (Day 86): it was the *median* in one study with a range of 18–254 days.
- **'Bystander effect: crowds don't help'** (Day 17): oversimplified; people usually do help in real CCTV data.
- **'Silence makes people talk'** (Day 36): no solid source found.
- **Duplicates removed:** Day 50 (chameleon effect) repeated Day 31 (mimicry).

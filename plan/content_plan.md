# Content plan — The Psyche Discourse

**Promise:** *Why your mind does what it does. A new short every day.* Bite-sized, research-backed everyday psychology. Faceless: hand-drawn stickman + captions + a free offline voice.

## Pillars -> three months
| Month | Days | Pillar / theme | Audience question |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-30 | **Mind tricks & cognitive biases** — how your brain fools you in daily decisions, memory and money | "Why did I do that?" |
| 2 | 31-60 | **Social & relationship psychology** — persuasion, influence, attraction, hidden rules of interaction | "Why do people act like that?" |
| 3 | 61-90 | **Habits, emotions & self-improvement** — practical psychology for focus, motivation and well-being | "How do I use this?" |

## Editorial rules (from CLAUDE.md)
1. Every spoken sentence traces to a named source (table per script). Contested claims are hedged on screen *and* in the voiceover.
2. We say when a famous claim is disputed (66-day habits, marshmallow test, Stanford prison experiment, Dunning-Kruger...). That "here's the catch" honesty is the channel's differentiator.
3. We never invent studies, numbers or researchers. Anything unverifiable is replaced (see `topic_review.md`).
4. No diagnosing, no therapy advice: practical tips are phrased as "researchers tested..." with a source.

## Episode template (45-52 s, ~125-140 words)
1. **Hook (0-3 s)** — a "you" question; makes no factual claim.
2. **Explanation (3-35 s)** — the concept, the named study (authors, year, what they did, what they found), why it happens.
3. **Catch / caveat** — replication, critique or limit, whenever one exists.
4. **Takeaway (35-45 s)** — one concrete thing to try, tied to a source.
5. **Teaser (45-50 s)** — "Tomorrow: ...?" a question about the next topic (no claim).

## Rotating angles (so the feed doesn't feel repetitive)
Study story · Myth vs. research · Everyday example · "Here's the catch" (contested) · Practical how-to · Mind-trick demo.

## Cadence & calendar
One Short per day, 90 days. `calendar.csv` assumes **Day 1 = Tue 6 Oct 2026** and daily publishing (change `schedule.start_date` in `channel_kit/config.json`; posting time is a placeholder: 18:00 local — owner to confirm). Videos are produced in weekly batches of 7 and QC'd before delivery.

## Metadata standards
- Title < 60 characters, curiosity-driven but never claims more than the research shows.
- Description: 2-3 sentences + CTA + source citation(s). 5-8 hashtags incl. #psychology #shorts #thepsychediscourse.
- Thumbnail: 1080x1920, big headline, one drawing, logo, day number.

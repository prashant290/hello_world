## Caveats / contested points

MIXED REPLICATION. The replications cited (Hesselmann 2020, plus two earlier attempts he reports) tested the computer-priming ('Google Stroop') claim, not the 'recall where to find it' finding; we do not know from these sources whether the latter replicated. The video avoids claiming search engines harm memory.

## Caveats / contested points

CONTESTED IN SCOPE. The widely repeated Economist-subscription example is not used because its data could not be verified. Frederick, Lee & Baskin (2014) say the attraction effect may be restricted to stylized, numeric product descriptions; it does not claim it never happens elsewhere.

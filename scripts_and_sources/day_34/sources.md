## Caveats / contested points

CONTESTED. The 65% figure is from one 1963 condition (Yale, 40 men). Perry (2013) draws on Milgram's archive: 24 variations, most with majority disobedience, improvised prompts, and some participants doubting the shocks were real. Haslam, Reicher & Birney (2014) propose 'engaged followership' based on an experimental analogue. The shocks were not real (the learner was an actor); described in neutral terms.

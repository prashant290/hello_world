## Caveats / contested points

Survey figure (89.2% weekly, N=12,519) is from Finnish internet users and may not generalize. The gum result is from small lab experiments (Experiment 1: 98 people) and is not a guaranteed cure. Jakubowski et al. compared tunes named as earworms with other songs; it describes tendencies, not rules.

## Caveats / contested points

CONTESTED. 'About twice' reflects a model parameter (2.25) fitted to certain gambling choices (Tversky & Kahneman 1992). Gal & Rucker (2018) argue evidence does not support losses being more impactful on balance and that context matters; defenders of loss aversion disagree. Video presents it as a debated rule of thumb.

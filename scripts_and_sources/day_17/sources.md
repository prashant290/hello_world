## Caveats / contested points

CONTESTED/OVERSIMPLIFIED. Darley & Latané percentages (about 85% vs about 31%) come from secondary summaries. Philpot et al. studied aggressive public conflicts in three cities; 'intervened' means at least one bystander acted. The '38 witnesses' Genovese story is not supported by court and police records (Manning et al. 2007).

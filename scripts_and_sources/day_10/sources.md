## Caveats / contested points

CONTESTED / MIXED. Radvansky et al. (2011) found the effect in real and virtual rooms; McFadyen et al. (2021) found no significant overall effect across four experiments, only increased memory errors under a working-memory load. No 'fix' is claimed, because later research found the effect even after returning to the original room (not claimed here for lack of a single verified primary source).

## Caveats / contested points

CONTESTED. Gignac & Zajenkowski (2020) used intelligence tests in 929 community participants; their critique applies to that way of testing, and Dunning and others dispute it. The popular 'Mount Stupid' graph is not from the 1999 paper (not used here).

## Caveats / contested points

CONTESTED. Danziger et al. (2011) is a correlational study of Israeli parole boards; Weinshall-Margel & Shapard argue case ordering explains the pattern. Hagger et al. (2016) tested ego depletion, a related but different idea (willpower as a limited resource), not decisions specifically. The video concludes only that decision fatigue is not settled.

## Caveats / contested points

Fischhoff's 1975 findings are from lab judgment tasks; Roese & Vohs (2012) review the broader literature and propose three levels of hindsight bias. The video's takeaway about checking written predictions is an application of the memory-distortion level, not a tested intervention.

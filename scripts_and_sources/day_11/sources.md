## Caveats / contested points

INFORMAL TERM. 'Frequency illusion' and 'Baader-Meinhof phenomenon' come from a linguist's blog and a newspaper letter, not from lab experiments. The explanation (attention and noticing) is a widely offered idea, not directly tested in the cited studies; Simons & Chabris demonstrates limits of attention, not the frequency illusion itself. The video hedges with 'one idea'.

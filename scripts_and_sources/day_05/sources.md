## Caveats / contested points

Zeigarnik's original memory claim has failed to replicate (Van Bergen 1968; Ghibellini & Meier 2025 meta-analysis). Masicampo & Baumeister (2011) studied goal-related intrusive thoughts in lab tasks, not everyday to-do lists.

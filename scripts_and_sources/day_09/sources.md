## Caveats / contested points

Figures are medians from the original wheel-of-fortune experiment (25 and 45). Anchoring is a robust finding, but effect size varies by task. Galinsky & Mussweiler studied simulated negotiations. The video does not claim that stores' 'original prices' work exactly this way; it presents the lab result and the negotiation research.

## Caveats / contested points

Stivers et al. (2009) measured question-answer timing in ten languages; Koudenburg et al. (2011) tested brief silences in group conversations in the lab. Neither shows silence 'makes people talk' (the original topic idea, dropped as unsupported). The takeaway links the two findings loosely and is hedged.

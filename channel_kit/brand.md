# The Psyche Discourse — channel kit

- **Name:** The Psyche Discourse  
- **Tagline:** Why your mind does what it does. A new short every day.  
- **Niche:** everyday psychology; bite-sized, research-backed explanations. Faceless.
- **Pillars:** (1) mind tricks & cognitive biases (2) social & relationship psychology (3) habits, emotions & self-improvement
- **Palette:** background sage `#DDE6E3`, ink navy `#1F2D3D`, accent orange `#E0A458` (caption highlight `#D9822B`). Source of truth: `config.json`.
- **Type:** Liberation Sans Bold (bundled in `fonts/`; free, open license). Brand mock used Lora (titles) + Poppins (body) — swap in if the owner supplies the files.
- **Logo:** `logo.png` is a *recreation* of the owner's brain mark (made by `src/make_logo.py`). Replace with the original file when available.
- **Voice:** Kokoro `am_michael` (free, offline). **Music:** synthesized in `src/make_music.py` (no licensing issues).
- **Video:** 1080x1920, 30 fps, 45-52 s, captions in the lower third above the bottom-20% UI zone.
- **Description template:** 2-3 sentences + "New psychology short every day on The Psyche Discourse." + source citation(s).
- **Hashtags:** 5-8 per video including #psychology #shorts #thepsychediscourse.
- **Tone:** curious, conversational, second person; every claim sourced; contested claims hedged on screen and in the voiceover.
- **Upload sheet:** `upload_metadata.csv` (titles, descriptions, hashtags, files). Posting time is a placeholder (config.json -> schedule).
